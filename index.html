A letter

Dil se, sirf tumhare liye

Meri Nisha,

Aaj tumhara din hai, aur sach bolun toh meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa tum ho. 2015 se aaj tak, 11 saal ka saath.

Main jaanta hoon main bahot gussail hoon. Gusse mein maine mobile tak tode hain. Main propose ki date bhool gaya, anniversary bhool gaya, kitni hi dates bhoolta aaya hoon. Phir bhi tum kabhi mera saath chhodkar nahi gayi. Mera gussa bhi tumne sambhala, meri galtiyan bhi.

Aaj main tumse promise karta hoon: apne gusse par kaam karunga, aur wo har date yaad rakhunga jo tumhare liye matter karti hai. 17 October aur 24 February to ab kabhi nahi bhoolunga.

Tumne mujhe non-veg se mana kiya tha. Ab ye meri khud ki choice ban chuki hai, aur main ise nibhaunga. Par agar kabhi kahin galti ho jaye, toh please mujhe maaf kar dena.

Tumhare sabr aur pyaar ke liye dil se shukriya. Bhagwan kare ye saal tumhe saari khushiyan aur sukoon de.

Hamesha tumhara, Anurag
